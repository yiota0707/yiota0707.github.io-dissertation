Assortative Matching and the Emergence of Cooperation

An interactive demonstration of the Baseline and Hybrid multi-agent reinforcement-learning models.

Both simulations contain 20 reinforcement-learning agents playing a repeated Prisoner’s Dilemma. The Baseline repeatedly reforms partnerships using random or assortative rematching. The Hybrid additionally learns whether existing partnerships should be retained or dissolved.
