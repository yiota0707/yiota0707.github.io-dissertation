WHAT YOU ARE WATCHING

Same learning environment. Different relationship mechanism.

Baseline

Every round reconstructs the partnerships. With probability m), rematching groups agents according to their previous PD behaviour. Otherwise pairing is random.

Hybrid

Agents first choose stay or switch. A pair survives only when both select stay. Dissolved pairs enter the same random or assortative rematching procedure.
